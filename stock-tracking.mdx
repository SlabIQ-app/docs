---
title: "Stock Tracking"
---

The **Stock Taking** feature allows you to perform a **full reconciliation** of your inventory, eBay listings, and orders.\
It helps ensure that your inventory data matches your actual stock and marketplace listings, reducing errors and preventing overselling.

---

## Key Features

- ✅ Compare physical inventory vs. SlabIQ records
- 🔄 Sync with eBay listings and order history
- 📊 Identify discrepancies, missing items, or duplicates
- 💡 Generate reports for audits and accounting

> Regular stock taking ensures accurate inventory, faster order fulfillment, and reliable financial reporting.

---

## Performing a Stock Take

### 1️⃣ Navigate to Stock Taking

Go to **Inventory → Stock Taking** to begin a reconciliation session.

---

### 2️⃣ Select Scope

Choose which items to include in your stock take:

- Entire inventory
- Specific batches or lots
- Specific categories, players, or teams

> ⚡ Tip: For large collections, stock take by batch or category to make the process more manageable.

---

### 3️⃣ Reconciliation Process

1. SlabIQ fetches current inventory and eBay listings
2. Compare **physical stock** vs. **digital records**
3. Identify discrepancies:

| Item             | Inventory Count | eBay Listed | Difference | Action                 |
| :--------------- | :-------------- | :---------- | :--------- | :--------------------- |
| LeBron James #23 | 5               | 5           | 0          | ✅ Balanced             |
| Luka Doncic #77  | 2               | 3           | -1         | ⚠ Missing in inventory |
| Rookie Lot – Jan | 10              | 10          | 0          | ✅ Balanced             |

4. Take corrective action:
   - Adjust inventory counts
   - Update listings
   - Investigate missing items

---

### 4️⃣ Stock Take Reports

After reconciliation, SlabIQ generates a **Stock Take Report**:

- Summary of total items, discrepancies, and adjustments
- Exportable in **CSV or PDF**
- Track historical stock takes for audits

---

## Best Practices

- Perform stock taking **weekly** or after large shipments
- Always reconcile **batches before publishing new listings**
- Track missing or excess inventory immediately to prevent overselling
- Combine stock taking with **Profit & Loss** and **Inventory Aging** reports to evaluate financial impact

---

### 5️⃣ Troubleshooting

**Discrepancies detected?**

- Verify items were scanned correctly or updated in SlabIQ
- Check for marketplace sync errors or missing eBay orders
- Re-run reconciliation after adjustments
- Contact support for unresolved inconsistencies

---

### 6️⃣ Pro Tips

- Use **batch scanning** to speed up stock taking for large collections
- Archive completed stock takes to maintain a clean audit trail
- Schedule stock takes before major sales events or promotions
