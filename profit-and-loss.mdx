The **Profit & Loss (P&L)** section helps you understand the financial performance of your inventory and sales.

SlabIQ tracks your revenue, expenses, and profit so you can quickly see what’s earning money and where costs are impacting your margins.

---

## Key Components

### 1️⃣ Revenue

Revenue represents the **total income** generated from sales.

- Includes:
  - Marketplace sales (eBay, Whatnot, etc.)
  - Direct sales through integrated stores
- Calculated automatically when a sale is marked complete

> 💡 Tip: For accurate revenue tracking, ensure all items sold are logged in SlabIQ with the correct sale price.

---

### 2️⃣ Expenses

Expenses represent the **costs associated with acquiring or selling your inventory**.

- Common examples:
  - Purchase price of slabs or cards
  - Grading fees (PSA, BGS, CGC)
  - Shipping and handling
  - Marketplace or transaction fees

Expenses are subtracted from revenue to calculate **profit**.

> ⚡ Tip: Always enter grading and shipping costs when adding items to your inventory for accurate P&L calculations.

---

### 3️⃣ Profit

Profit is the **difference between revenue and expenses**.

**Formula:**

```
Profit = Revenue – Expenses
```

SlabIQ displays both **gross profit** (before fees) and **net profit** (after fees and optional adjustments).

> ✅ Helps you identify which items, batches, or lots are most profitable.

## Best Practices

✔ Always log **purchase price and fees** for every item\
 ✔ Use batches and lots to track profitability per group\
 ✔ Regularly review P&L trends to identify high-margin items\
 ✔ Adjust pricing or sourcing strategies based on insights

---

## Advanced Tips

- Combine **P&L insights with Analytics** to see which player, team, or set drives the most profit
- Export P&L reports for tax purposes or performance tracking
- Compare revenue and profit across different marketplaces to optimize your selling strategy
