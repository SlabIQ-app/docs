The **Profit & Loss Dashboard** gives you a visual snapshot of your revenue, expenses, and profit.\
Charts and tables make it easy to spot trends, high-performing items, and areas to optimize.

## Filters and Customization

SlabIQ allows you to filter charts by:

- Date range (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Item, batch, or lot
- Player, team, sport, or set
- Marketplace

> 🔧 Tip: Use filters to identify **seasonal trends**, high-margin items, or underperforming categories.

---

## Export and Reporting

You can export:

- Charts as PNGs or PDFs
- Full P&L tables as CSV
- Filtered reports for tax or accounting

> 💡 Pro tip: Regularly export P&L snapshots to track growth over time.

---

## Best Practices

- Review **Revenue vs. Expenses** weekly to track margins
- Compare **Profit by Batch/Lot** to identify top-performing workflows
- Use **Marketplace Comparison** to adjust where you list items
- Combine with **Inventory Analytics** to plan purchases strategically
