title: "Inventory Aging"
---

The **Inventory Aging** feature helps you track how long each item has been in your inventory.\
Knowing the age of your inventory allows you to optimize turnover, improve cash flow, and identify items that may need repricing or promotion.

---

## How Inventory Aging Works

SlabIQ calculates inventory age using the **date the item was added** to your system.

- Age = Current Date – Date Added
- Displayed in **days, weeks, or months**
- Can be filtered by **player, team, set, or category**

> 💡 Tip: Use inventory age to prioritize selling older items first or to identify slow-moving inventory.

---

## Inventory Aging Dashboard

SlabIQ provides visualizations to quickly assess inventory age:

### 1️⃣ Aging Buckets

Items are grouped into aging buckets such as:

| Age         | Description                       |
| :---------- | :-------------------------------- |
| 0–30 days   | New arrivals                      |
| 31–90 days  | Recently held                     |
| 91–180 days | Moderate inventory age            |
| 181+ days   | Long-term inventory / slow movers |

> 🔹 This helps identify items that may need price adjustments or promotion.

## Filters and Sorting

You can filter or sort inventory by:

- Player / Team / Set
- Grading / Condition
- Lot / Batch
- Marketplace status
- Days in inventory

> 🔧 Tip: Filter to see all items older than 90 days to plan clearance or promotions.

---

## Best Practices

- Monitor **90+ day inventory** weekly
- Adjust pricing or promote slow-moving items
- Combine with **Profit & Loss** to determine if older items are still profitable
- Use **Lot Builder** to bundle older items for faster turnover

---

## Example Use Cases

1. **Identify slow movers** – Items sitting over 180 days may need a discount or bundle.
2. **Plan promotions** – Highlight older inventory for seasonal sales or marketplace boosts.
3. **Improve cash flow** – Prioritize selling older high-value items first.
