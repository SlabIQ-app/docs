---
title: "Collections & Binders"
---

The **Collections & Binders** feature allows you to **organize your inventory** into virtual collections or binders, giving you a structured and visual way to manage your cards or collectibles.

Whether you’re a collector, seller, or flipper, this feature helps you track items, plan for sales, and organize your inventory efficiently.

---

## Key Features

- 📂 **Create Collections** for categories such as sport, player, year, or set
- 📖 **Create Binders** to organize physical or virtual groupings
- 🔄 Track inventory items within collections or binders
- 💡 Integrate with **Pick List, Lot Builder, and Templates** for easier selling

> Collections & Binders give a **visual and logical structure** to your inventory for better management.

---

## Creating Collections

1. Navigate to **Inventory → Collections**
2. Click **“Create Collection”**
3. Enter a name and description
4. Optionally assign categories such as **Sport, Team, or Year**
5. Add items manually or via batch selection

📊 **Example Collection Table:**

| Collection Name   | Category   | Items | Notes               |
| :---------------- | :--------- | :---- | :------------------ |
| Basketball 2023   | Basketball | 120   | PSA Graded & Raw    |
| Rookie Cards 2023 | Basketball | 50    | PSA 9–10            |
| Autographed Sets  | Basketball | 30    | Autographs included |

---

## Creating Binders

- Navigate to **Inventory → Binders**
- Click **“Create Binder”**
- Assign a **name, location, and capacity**
- Add items manually, via collection, or with batch selection

📊 **Example Binder Table:**

| Binder Name        | Location | Capacity | Items |
| :----------------- | :------- | :------- | :---- |
| Box 1 – Basketball | Shelf A  | 50       | 45    |
| Box 2 – Football   | Shelf B  | 40       | 32    |
| PSA Graded         | Shelf C  | 30       | 30    |

> 🔹 Binders are ideal for **physical storage organization** while Collections can be **virtual groupings** for sales or analytics.

---

## Integration With Other Features

- **Pick List:** Easily pull items from a collection or binder for shipping
- **Lot Builder:** Create lots from a collection or binder
- **Inventory Aging & P&L:** Track performance of items within collections or binders
- **Templates & Cross Listing:** Apply templates or list entire collections

---

## Best Practices

- Organize **Collections** by theme, player, set, or year for easy tracking
- Use **Binders** to match physical storage locations for efficient picking
- Combine with **Inventory Aging** to prioritize older items
- Regularly update items as they are graded, sold, or acquired

---

### Example Workflow

1. Create a **“Rookie Cards 2023” collection**
2. Group all relevant items into a **Binder on Shelf A**
3. Use the Pick List to select items from the collection for a **Whatnot sale**
4. Track profits and inventory aging for this collection in SlabIQ
