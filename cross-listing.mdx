title: "Cross Listing"
---

The **Cross Listing** section lets you review and manage your inventory across multiple marketplaces.\
It provides insights into which items are listed, where, and how efficiently you are using your SlabIQ integrations.

---

## Key Features

- ✅ View all items listed across marketplaces
- 🔄 Track listing status (Active, Sold, Pending)
- 📊 Identify items **not yet listed**
- 💡 Monitor listing performance for each marketplace

> Cross Listing helps ensure your inventory reaches the widest audience without duplication errors.

---

## Cross Listing Dashboard

The dashboard provides:

- **Marketplace Overview** – See how many items are listed per platform
- **Listing Status** – Active, Draft, Pending, or Sold
- **Revenue Potential** – Sum of listed item prices per marketplace
- **Integration Health** – Verify connected accounts are active and syncing

### 3️⃣ Cross Listing Efficiency Metrics

SlabIQ calculates metrics like:

- **Percentage of inventory cross-listed** – How many items appear on multiple marketplaces
- **Average days to sale per marketplace** – Track which platform sells faster
- **Revenue per marketplace** – Compare where your sales are highest

📊 **Example Metrics:**

- 75% of inventory cross-listed
- Average days to sale: eBay 7 days, Whatnot 10 days
- Highest revenue: eBay (\$15k), Whatnot (\$8.5k)

> Metrics help you optimize listings and marketplace strategy.

---

### 4️⃣ Best Practices

- Ensure **all connected marketplaces** are syncing correctly
- Review items not listed on all relevant marketplaces
- Adjust pricing based on performance per marketplace
- Use **Lot Builder** and **Templates** to speed up cross-listing
- Regularly monitor revenue potential vs. active listings

---

### 5️⃣ Troubleshooting

**Items not appearing on a marketplace?**

- Check integration status under **Settings → Integrations**
- Make sure the item meets marketplace requirements (listing limits, category, condition)
- Re-sync or re-publish the listing if needed

**Marketplace not updating?**

- Verify API authorization is active
- Refresh the integration connection
- Contact SlabIQ support if issues persist
