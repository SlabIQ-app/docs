---
title: "Profile"
---

The **Profile** section in **Settings** allows you to manage your **personal account information** and customize your experience in SlabIQ.

Keeping your profile up-to-date ensures smooth account access, accurate communication, and integration with connected marketplaces.

---

## Key Features

- 🖊 **Edit Personal Information:** Name, email, phone number
- 🔒 **Change Password:** Secure your account with a new password
- 🌐 **Manage Connected Accounts:** Link or unlink marketplace accounts (eBay, Whatnot, Shopify, etc.)
- 📧 **Notification Preferences:** Opt-in for emails about sales, inventory alerts, or promotions
- 🖼 **Profile Picture:** Upload an avatar for your account

---

## Updating Your Profile

1. Navigate to **Settings → Profile**
2. Click **Edit** next to the field you want to update
3. Make changes and click **Save**

📋 **Example Fields:**

| Field              | Description                                    |
| :----------------- | :--------------------------------------------- |
| Name               | Display name for your account                  |
| Email              | Primary email used for notifications and login |
| Phone              | Optional phone number for SMS notifications    |
| Profile Picture    | Upload an avatar to personalize your account   |
| Password           | Change your login password                     |
| Connected Accounts | eBay, Whatnot, Shopify, and other integrations |
| Notifications      | Email, SMS, or in-app alerts                   |

---

## Connected Accounts

SlabIQ lets you manage all marketplace connections directly from your profile:

- View all linked accounts
- Connect new accounts (eBay, Whatnot, Shopify)
- Disconnect inactive or outdated accounts

> 🔹 Keeping marketplace connections current ensures accurate **cross-listing, inventory, and P&L tracking**.

---

## Notification Preferences

- Toggle on/off for email or SMS alerts:
  - Sales notifications
  - Inventory aging alerts
  - Cross-listing reminders
  - System updates and promotions

> 💡 Tip: Enable notifications for **inventory or sold item alerts** to manage your workflow efficiently.

---

## Best Practices

- Keep your **email and phone number up-to-date** for critical notifications
- Regularly review **connected accounts** to prevent sync issues
- Update your **password periodically** for security
- Personalize your profile picture for easier account recognition in team environments
