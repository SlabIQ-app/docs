title: "Search & Comp Checker"
---

The **Search & Comp Checker** allows you to quickly determine the **current market value** of your cards or collectibles.\
You can search by image, take a photo, or enter a certification number, and SlabIQ will return recent sales and current pricing trends.

---

## How It Works

SlabIQ provides three ways to perform a search:

### 1️⃣ Upload an Image

- Drag and drop a card image or browse to upload
- SlabIQ analyzes the image and matches it to known cards
- Retrieves recent sales, listings, and current market prices

### 2️⃣ Take a Photo on Your Phone

- Use your phone camera to scan the card
- SlabIQ performs **image recognition** in real-time
- Provides instant market comps

### 3️⃣ Enter Certification Number

- Input PSA, BGS, or CGC cert number
- SlabIQ fetches **exact card information**
- Displays historical sale prices and market listings

## Pricing Insights

SlabIQ calculates:

- **Market Average Price** – Mean price from recent sales and active listings
- **Price Range** – High / Low of recent transactions
- **Trends** – Shows if the card is trending up or down

## Best Practices

- Use **images for accurate recognition**, especially for ungraded or generic cards
- Enter **cert numbers for exact comps**
- Cross-check comps across multiple marketplaces for reliability
- Combine with **Inventory & Pricing** pages to adjust your listings

---

## Example Workflow

1. User uploads an image of a LeBron James rookie card
2. SlabIQ detects the card: 2023 Prizm, #23, PSA 10
3. Comp Checker displays recent sales: \$500, \$520, \$480
4. Market Average calculated: \$510
5. Suggested listing price: \$510–\$520 based on market trends
