---
title: "Inventory Supply & Cost"
---

The **Inventory Supply & Costs** section allows you to monitor **inventory quantities** and **associated costs** over time.\
This helps you make informed decisions about purchasing, pricing, and stock management.

---

## Key Components

### 1️⃣ Inventory Quantities

SlabIQ tracks:

- Total items in stock
- Items per category, player, team, or set
- Batch or lot-level quantities
- Marketplace-specific availability

📊 **Example: Inventory by Category**

| Category   | Quantity |
| :--------- | :------- |
| Basketball | 120      |
| Football   | 85       |
| Baseball   | 60       |
| Other      | 30       |

> Use this to monitor stock levels and plan replenishment or sales campaigns.

---

### 2️⃣ Acquisition Costs

SlabIQ records **all costs** associated with each item:

- Purchase price
- Grading fees
- Shipping and handling
- Marketplace fees (optional)

📊 **Example: Average Cost per Category**

| Category   | Avg. Purchase Cost | Avg. Fees | Total Avg. Cost |
| :--------- | :----------------- | :-------- | :-------------- |
| Basketball | \$80               | \$20      | \$100           |
| Football   | \$60               | \$15      | \$75            |
| Baseball   | \$50               | \$10      | \$60            |

> Knowing acquisition costs helps calculate profit accurately and set realistic pricing.

---

### 4️⃣ Filters and Sorting

SlabIQ allows filtering by:

- Player, team, set, or category
- Batch or lot
- Marketplace
- Date added or purchase date

> Use filters to track trends for specific items or groups.

---

### 5️⃣ Reports & Export

- Export inventory quantities and costs as **CSV or PDF**
- Compare inventory supply and costs across multiple periods
- Track cost evolution for budgeting and purchasing strategy

---

### Best Practices

- Track **costs accurately** when adding new items to inventory
- Monitor **supply vs. demand** to avoid overstocking or stockouts
- Combine with **Profit & Loss** and **Inventory Aging** for holistic insights
- Review trends monthly to optimize purchasing and pricing strategies
