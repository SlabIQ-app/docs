---
title: "Branding"
---

The **Branding** section allows you to **add your personal or business branding** to your listings, images, and card scans.\
This helps maintain a consistent professional appearance across marketplaces and gives your inventory a recognizable identity.

---

## Key Features

- 🖼 **Logo Upload:** Add your logo to appear on images, scans, and templates
- 🎨 **Watermark Settings:** Apply watermarks to card photos and scans for protection
- ✏️ **Custom Text:** Add your business name, slogan, or disclaimers
- 🔄 **Apply to Templates:** Automatically include branding on **title/description templates** or batch uploads

---

## Adding Branding Images

1. Navigate to **Settings → Branding**
2. Click **Upload Logo / Watermark**
3. Choose file (PNG or JPEG recommended for transparency and quality)
4. Adjust **size, opacity, and position** for watermarking
5. Save settings — branding will automatically apply to new listings and scans

---

## Example Branding Table

| Branding Type | File             | Placement                | Notes                                 |
| :------------ | :--------------- | :----------------------- | :------------------------------------ |
| Logo          | logo.png         | Top-right of scan        | Appears on exported card scans        |
| Watermark     | watermark.png    | Center, semi-transparent | Protects images from unauthorized use |
| Custom Text   | “Tristate Cards” | Bottom-left              | Appears on eBay listing images        |

> 💡 Tip: Use subtle watermarks to **protect images without obscuring card details**.

---

## Integration With Other Features

- **Templates:** Apply logos or watermarks automatically to all listings created with title/description templates
- **Comp Checker / Pick List:** Branding can appear on exported reports or card scan images
- **Inventory & Batches:** Batch uploads will automatically include configured branding

---

## Best Practices

- Use **transparent PNGs** for clean overlays
- Position logos/watermarks where they don’t obscure card details
- Keep branding consistent across all marketplaces for a **professional look**
- Update branding periodically to reflect business changes or promotions

---

### Example Workflow

1. Upload a logo in **Settings → Branding**
2. Apply a watermark with your business name to scans
3. Save and preview how images appear in listings
4. All new card uploads and batch listings will automatically include branding
