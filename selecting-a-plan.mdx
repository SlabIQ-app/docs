SlabIQ offers **simple, transparent pricing** designed for everyone from casual hobbyists to serious collectors and sellers. You can start free and upgrade at any time to unlock more scans and advanced features.

---

## 🧾 Overview of SlabIQ Plans

Below are the current SlabIQ subscription tiers:

### 🆓 **Free**

**Best for:** New collectors who want to explore SlabIQ’s core features and track collection value.

- 💸 \$0 / month
- 🔍 Up to **10 scans per month**
- 📊 Price tracking & eBay integration
- 📦 Collection management
- 📁 Binder creator
- 📈 Unlimited inventory size

👉 Good choice if you’re just getting started or have a small collection.

---

### 📈 **Starter — \$9.99/month**

**Best for:** Casual collectors who scan more often.

- 📸 **500 scans/month**
- Basic and batch scanning
- Inventory tracking
- Price alerts
- eBay import
- Plus everything in Free Plan

👉 Provides more scans and tools for regular hobby use, without a big commitment.

---

### 🚀 **Pro — \$24.99/month**

**Best for:** Serious collectors and sellers.

- 📸 **1000 scans/month**
- AI-powered recognition
- Unlimited inventory
- Price history and alerts
- eBay sync
- Marketplace views
- Export tools
- Sales Reports
- Plus everythiing in Starter Plan

👉 Ideal if you scan frequently, sell cards, or want deeper market insights.

## 🧠 How to Choose the Right Plan

Here’s a quick guide to help you decide:

| **Goal**                           | **Recommended Plan** |
| :--------------------------------- | :------------------- |
| Just exploring SlabIQ              | Free                 |
| Regular scanning & alerts          | Starter              |
| Full analytics + marketplace tools | Pro                  |

> 👍 You can upgrade or downgrade at any time — your data stays safe no matter the plan.

---

## 🔄 Switching Plans

To change your subscription:

1. Log in to SlabIQ
2. Open your account settings
3. Go to **Billing & Subscription**
4. Choose a new plan
5. Confirm your update

🎯 Upgrades take effect immediately.\
🔁 Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.

---

## 💡 Tips for Plan Selection

- **Start free:** Try the _Free_ plan to see how SlabIQ fits your workflow.
- **Scan priority:** If you’re scanning mid-to-high volume, _Starter_ or _Pro_ gives you more monthly capacity.
- **Value for sellers:** _Pro_ unlocks eBay sync, full export, and historical insights — great for flip tracking and ROI analysis.

---

## 📌 How Billing Works

- Plans are billed monthly.
- You can cancel or switch anytime from the dashboard.
- Your inventory and historical data remain intact across plan changes.
