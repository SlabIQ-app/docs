---
title: "Appearance"
---

The **Appearance** section allows you to **customize the visual style** of SlabIQ to match your preferences.\
You can adjust themes, colors, and display options to improve readability and overall user experience.

---

## Key Features

- 🌗 **Theme Selection:** Switch between Light and Dark mode
- 🎨 **Accent Color:** Customize highlight colors across the platform
- 🖋 **Font Size & Style:** Adjust for readability and accessibility
- 🖼 **Dashboard Layout:** Optional display configurations for widgets and panels

---

## Theme Selection

- **Light Mode:** Bright interface for well-lit environments
- **Dark Mode:** Reduced eye strain for low-light or night use

> ⚡ Tip: Dark mode can save battery on devices with OLED screens.

---

## Accent Color

- Change **highlight and accent colors** throughout SlabIQ
- Choose from preset colors or a custom hex value
- Accent color affects buttons, links, charts, and selected items

📊 **Example:**

- Red for alerts or high-priority items
- Green for profits or positive trends
- Blue for informational highlights

---

## Font & Display Options

- Adjust **font size** for easier readability
- Choose **compact or spacious layout** for dashboards and tables
- Optimize for your device screen size

---

## Best Practices

- Use **contrastive accent colors** for important alerts and metrics
- Combine **Dark Mode** with softer accent colors to reduce eye strain
- Adjust font size to suit your workflow for long inventory or reporting sessions
- Experiment with dashboard layouts for your most-used pages (Inventory, P&L, Pick List)

---

## Example Workflow

1. Navigate to **Settings → Appearance**
2. Switch to **Dark Mode** for night-time work
3. Change accent color to **Red** for high-priority notifications
4. Adjust font size to **Medium** for comfortable reading
5. Save settings — your SlabIQ interface updates instantly
