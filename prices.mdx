The **Prices** section allows you to analyze **pricing trends** for your inventory before and after sales.\
It helps you determine optimal listing prices, track market value changes, and evaluate your profitability over time.

---

## Key Components

### 1️⃣ Pre-Sale Pricing

SlabIQ helps you set prices before listing:

- 💵 **Suggested Price** – Based on recent sales, market trends, and comparable listings
- 📊 **Price Range Analysis** – See high, low, and average prices for similar items
- ⚡ **Dynamic Pricing Alerts** – Notifies you when market value changes

> Setting a competitive pre-sale price increases the likelihood of a fast sale and maximizes revenue.

---

### 2️⃣ Post-Sale Pricing

After an item sells, SlabIQ tracks:

- Final **Sale Price**
- **Price vs. Suggested Price** – Compare actual sale price to pre-sale expectations
- **Profit Impact** – Understand how pricing affected net profit

---

### 5️⃣ Pricing Alerts & Recommendations

- **Overpriced Items** – Alerts if items exceed market range, reducing sell-through
- **Underpriced Items** – Alerts if items are below average, protecting potential profit
- **Batch Recommendations** – Suggest pricing adjustments for entire batches or lots

---

## Best Practices

✔ Regularly review **pre-sale suggested prices** before listing\
 ✔ Compare **actual sale price vs. suggested price** to refine future pricing\
 ✔ Adjust slow-moving inventory prices using **Inventory Aging insights**\
 ✔ Use **cross-listing data** to optimize pricing across marketplaces

---

### 6️⃣ Advanced Tips

- Combine **Price Trends + P&L + Inventory Aging** to spot high-value items
- Track **seasonal trends** to anticipate price fluctuations
- Automate pricing for lots or batches using SlabIQ templates and market data
