title: "Sales History"
---

The **Sales History** section provides a complete record of all your sold items, including marketplace, price, fees, and profit.\
Tracking sales history allows you to analyze trends, measure profitability, and make informed decisions for future purchases and listings.

---

## Key Components of Sales History

### 1️⃣ Sale Details

For each sale, SlabIQ records:

- **Item Name / Lot Name** – The card or lot sold
- **Player / Team / Set** – Relevant metadata
- **Sale Price** – Final sale amount
- **Purchase Cost** – Original item cost
- **Fees** – Marketplace or shipping fees
- **Profit** – Automatically calculated (Revenue – Expenses)
- **Date Sold** – When the transaction completed
- **Marketplace** – eBay, Whatnot, Direct Sales, etc.

> 💡 Tip: Ensure all purchase costs and fees are entered for accurate profit calculations.

---

### 2️⃣ Viewing Sales History

1. Navigate to **Inventory → Sales History**
2. Use the **search bar** to locate a specific item or lot
3. Click a sale record for detailed transaction information

---

### 3️⃣ Filters and Sorting

SlabIQ allows you to filter and sort your sales history by:

- Date range (daily, weekly, monthly, custom)
- Player, team, or set
- Batch or lot
- Marketplace
- Sale price or profit

> 🔧 Tip: Use filters to identify top-performing items, categories, or time periods.

---

### 6️⃣ Exporting Sales History

- Export full history as **CSV** or **Excel** for accounting, taxes, or reporting
- Export filtered views for custom reports
- Include P&L fields for profit analysis

---

### 7️⃣ Best Practices

- Regularly review sales history to understand **what sells fast**
- Compare sale price to purchase cost and fees for **profit analysis**
- Track **seasonal trends** and plan inventory purchases accordingly
- Combine with **Inventory Aging** to identify items that have been slow-moving
