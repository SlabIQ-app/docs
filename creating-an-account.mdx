Welcome to **SlabIQ** 👋

Creating an account takes less than a minute and gives you full access to your cloud-based inventory, analytics, and valuation tools.

---

## Step 1: Visit SlabIQ

Go to:

👉 [**https://slabiq.app**](https://slabiq.app)

Click **Sign Up** in the top right corner.

---

## Step 2: Enter Your Information

Fill in the required fields:

- **Full Name**
- **Email Address**
- **Password**

 \<CreateAccountRequirements /\> 

### Password Requirements

Your password must:

- Be at least 8 characters
- Include at least one uppercase letter
- Include at least one lowercase letter
- Include at least one number

---

## Step 3: Verify Your Email

After signing up, you’ll receive a verification email.

1. Open the email from **SlabIQ**
2. Click **Verify Account**
3. You’ll be redirected back to the dashboard

> ⚠️ If you don’t see the email, check your spam or promotions folder.

---

## Step 4: Log In

Once verified:

1. Go back to [**https://slabiq.app**](https://slabiq.app)
2. Click **Login**
3. Enter your email and password

You’re now ready to start managing your inventory.

---

## Already Have an Account?

Click **Login** instead of Sign Up on the homepage.

If you forgot your password:

- Click **Forgot Password**
- Enter your email
- Follow the reset instructions

---

## What Happens Next?

After logging in for the first time, we recommend:

- Reviewing the **Dashboard Overview**
- Adding your **first inventory item**
- Exploring your analytics
