## Why Use SlabIQ?

Managing collectibles can be complicated. Between tracking inventory, analyzing market trends, listing items across multiple marketplaces, and keeping up with sales and shipments, it’s easy for things to get overwhelming. SlabIQ simplifies this process by offering:

- **Real-Time Inventory Management:** Keep your collection organized, track items across boxes and binders, and know exactly what’s in stock.
- **Powerful Sales Analytics:** Monitor revenue, profit, and pricing trends to make smarter decisions.
- **Marketplace Integrations:** List and cross-list effortlessly on eBay, Whatnot, and more.
- **Comp Checker & Search Tools:** Quickly determine market value using images, photos, or certification numbers.
- **Lot & Batch Management:** Build, manage, and ship lots with efficiency and accuracy.
- **Custom Templates & Branding:** Standardize listings with title and description templates, and add your own branding for a professional look.
- **Team Collaboration:** Invite collaborators, assign roles, and keep everyone on the same page.

SlabIQ is **designed for everyone in the collectibles ecosystem** — from casual collectors who want a clean, organized view of their collection, to professional flippers and sellers managing hundreds or thousands of listings across multiple marketplaces.
