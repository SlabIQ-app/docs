Welcome to **SlabIQ** — the smart way to manage, analyze, and document your slab grading workflows.

SlabIQ is designed to help users from hobbyists to professionals streamline grading, tracking, and valuation with clarity and confidence. This documentation will guide you through every step, starting from setup to advanced usage.

## 🚀 What Is SlabIQ?

SlabIQ is a powerful platform built to simplify slab management and grading insights. Whether you're cataloging collectibles, automating slab data, or generating reports, SlabIQ gives you the tools to:

- 📊 Analyze slab metrics and trends
- 🗂 Organize and search slab inventory
- 🔍 View slab details and history at a glance
- 📈 Generate customizable reports

Perfect for collectors, graders, and hobby businesses alike.
