title: "Subscription"
---

The **Subscription** section lets you **view, manage, and upgrade** your SlabIQ subscription.\
It provides an overview of your current plan, billing details, and available upgrades or add-ons.

---

## Key Features

- 📦 **Current Plan Overview:** See your active subscription plan
- 💳 **Billing Information:** Update credit card or payment method
- 🔄 **Upgrade / Downgrade:** Change your plan based on needs
- 📅 **Billing History:** Review past invoices and payment dates
- ⏱ **Renewal & Cancellation:** Manage automatic renewal or cancel subscription

---

## Updating Billing Information

1. Click **Edit Payment Method**
2. Enter new credit card or payment info
3. Save changes to update your billing automatically

> 🔹 Keeping payment information current prevents service interruptions.

---

## Upgrading or Downgrading Plans

- Click **Change Plan** to see available options
- Compare features of each plan
- Confirm to upgrade or downgrade immediately

> ⚡ Tip: Upgrading unlocks additional features such as **Marketplace Integrations, Lot Builder, or Advanced Analytics**.

## Renewal & Cancellation

- Automatic renewal is enabled by default
- Canceling stops future billing but retains access until the end of the current billing period
- You can re-activate or switch plans at any time

> 💡 Tip: Enable reminders for plan renewal if you want to review your features before billing.

---

## Best Practices

- Keep billing info up-to-date to avoid service disruption
- Review your plan features regularly to ensure your workflow is supported
- Download invoices for your financial records
- Consider upgrading if you need **additional integrations or advanced reporting**
