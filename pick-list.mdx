---
title: "Pick List"
---

The **Pick List** provides a **real-time list of all items that are sold and awaiting shipment**.\
It helps you organize and prepare items for fulfillment efficiently, ensuring orders are shipped accurately and on time.

Once a shipping label is created for an item, it is automatically **removed from the pick list**.

---

## Key Features

- ✅ Lists all **sold items awaiting shipping**
- 🔄 Automatically updates when shipping labels are created
- 📋 Sort and filter by **SKU, Box, Buyer, Marketplace, or Lot/Batch**
- 💡 Export pick lists for **fulfillment or auditing**

> The Pick List acts as your **shipping queue**, keeping fulfillment organized and error-free.

## Sorting & Filtering

SlabIQ allows you to **sort or filter pick lists** by:

- SKU or item number
- Box / storage location
- Buyer name
- Marketplace
- Lot / Batch

> ⚡ Tip: Use sorting by box to streamline physical picking for shipping.

---

## Workflow Example

1. Open **Pick List** to view all sold items awaiting shipment
2. Sort by **Buyer** to process multiple orders efficiently
3. Pick the items from storage according to **Box or SKU**
4. Create shipping labels for all items in the batch
5. Pick List automatically removes shipped items, keeping your queue clean

---

## Best Practices

- Regularly check the Pick List to ensure **orders are fulfilled promptly**
- Use **filters and sorting** to optimize picking workflow
- Export PDF or CSV if needed for **team fulfillment**
- Combine with **Inventory & Sales History** to track fulfillment progress
