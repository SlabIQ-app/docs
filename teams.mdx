title: "Teams"
---

The **Team** section allows you to **invite employees or collaborators** to help manage your SlabIQ account.\
You can assign roles, control access, and ensure your team has the right permissions to manage inventory, listings, and analytics efficiently.

---

## Key Features

- 👥 **Invite Team Members:** Add users via email
- 🏷 **Assign Roles:** Customize access levels based on responsibilities
- 🔄 **Manage Permissions:** Control what team members can view or edit
- 🗑 **Remove or Deactivate Members:** Keep team access up-to-date

---

## Inviting a Team Member

1. Navigate to **Settings → Team**
2. Click **Invite Member**
3. Enter the **team member’s email**
4. Select **role** (Owner, Manager, Viewer)
5. Send invitation — the user receives an email to join

---

## Current Roles & Permissions

| Role        | Permissions                                                                                               | Recommended Use                                                   |
| :---------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**   | • Full access to all features<br />• Manage team members<br />• Delete team<br />• Billing & subscription | Primary account holder or senior admin                            |
| **Manager** | • Edit cards & listings<br />• End/relist items<br />• Manage inventory<br />• Invite new members         | Operations lead or inventory manager                              |
| **Viewer**  | • View all cards & listings<br />• View inventory<br />• View analytics<br />• Read-only access           | Auditors, accountants, or team members who only need to view data |

> ⚡ Tip: Assign roles carefully to balance **control, collaboration, and security**.

---

## Managing Team Members

- View **active, pending, or deactivated members**
- Change **roles** at any time
- Remove team members who no longer need access

---

## Best Practices

- Invite only **trusted collaborators**
- Regularly review **roles and permissions**
- Use **Viewer role** for team members who do not need editing access
- Track team activity to ensure accountability

---

### Example Workflow

1. Owner invites a new employee via email
2. Assign the **Manager role** to handle inventory and listings
3. Employee logs in and sees only the features allowed by their role
4. Owner monitors team and adjusts roles as needed
